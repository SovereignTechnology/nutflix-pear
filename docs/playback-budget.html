Reading the estimate

Divide the file size by the block size and round up. Multiply by the price per block to get the full price. The budget covers whole blocks only. With the example values, the last byte starts block 17, raising the full price from 48 to 51 sats.

The split covers only the blocks within budget. For a fresh channel serving one core at the entered price, the creator receives the amount paid × creator percentage, rounded down. Nutflix carries fractional shares forward between accepted payments on that channel and core. The seeder receives the remaining whole sats. Mint fees, cached blocks and payments to other peers can change your actual wallet spend or the split.
